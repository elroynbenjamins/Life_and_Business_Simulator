## Full description

Build a life. Grow your wealth. Create your empire.

Life Empire is a detailed life and business simulation game where every week brings new financial decisions. Start with education and part-time work, build a career, manage your lifestyle, invest in the market, acquire real estate, and launch companies across multiple industries.

YOUR LIFE, YOUR STRATEGY

Choose an education, apply for jobs, earn promotions, and meet the housing and vehicle requirements needed to advance. Balance salary, taxes, rent, food, transport, and loan payments while building long-term financial security.

BUILD AND SCALE BUSINESSES

Start companies in a range of industries, inject working capital, recruit employees, train your team, manage morale, choose pricing and advertising strategies, and invest in upgrades. Reputation, market share, competitors, business events, loans, and international locations all influence growth and profitability.

INVEST FOR THE LONG TERM

Buy and sell stocks and commodities in a changing market. Follow news, market sentiment, sector events, dividends, realized gains, and portfolio statistics. You can also purchase property or compete against other bidders in real estate auctions.

CREATE A LASTING LEGACY

Complete achievements, earn Prestige Points, and unlock permanent bonuses for future playthroughs. Daily rewards and optional rewarded advertisements can provide gems, while optional purchases offer gem packs and permanent ad removal.

FEATURES

- Career paths, education, promotions, and salary progression
- Personal budgets, taxes, bank deposits, and loans
- Stocks, commodities, dividends, and market events
- Business ownership, employees, reputation, competitors, and expansion
- Real estate ownership and property auctions
- Achievements, Prestige upgrades, statistics, and multiple save slots
- Optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases

Life Empire is a fictional simulation game. Its markets, companies, returns, salaries, and financial outcomes are fictional and are not financial advice.

## Data safety draft

Verify these answers against the final Google Play SDK disclosure before submitting:

- Local game saves and settings are stored on the device.
- Google AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising data, diagnostics, and interaction data to provide and measure ads, subject to the user's consent choices.
- Google Play Billing processes purchases; the game stores product and transaction identifiers to prevent duplicate fulfillment.
- The app does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or financial account details.
- Data is encrypted in transit by the third-party Google services used by the app.
- Users can clear local game data by deleting save slots or uninstalling the app. Advertising privacy choices can be reviewed in Support.
