# Privacy Policy for Life Empire

Effective date: 23 August 2026

Life Empire is a fictional life, investing, real-estate, and business simulation game published by **Snelroy**, operated by **Elroy N Benjamins** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use Life Empire and how you can contact us about privacy.

## Scope

This policy applies to the Life Empire Android application, including its advertising, in-app purchasing, local save, and external-link features. Life Empire does not require an account and we do not operate a server that receives or stores your game saves.

## Information stored locally

Life Empire stores the following information locally on your device so the game can function:

- Save games, player-entered display name, progression, statistics, and settings;
- Achievements, Prestige progress, and virtual currency balances;
- Advertising-removal status, daily reward and rewarded-ad usage information;
- Product and transaction identifiers needed to deliver purchases, restore permanent entitlements, and prevent duplicate fulfillment; and
- Advertising consent status made available by Google's consent tools.

This locally stored information is not sent to a Snelroy or Life Empire server. You can delete individual save slots from the game. You can remove all locally stored information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

## Advertising and consent

Life Empire uses **Google AdMob** to provide optional rewarded advertisements and occasional interstitial advertisements. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, Google and its advertising partners may access or process:

- Device and advertising identifiers;
- IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address;
- Advertising interactions, impressions, and engagement data;
- Device, app, performance, crash, and diagnostic information; and
- Consent choices used to determine whether ads may be requested or personalized.

This information may be used to provide, secure, limit, personalize where permitted, and measure advertising. Where required, Life Empire displays Google's consent form before requesting advertisements. You can review or change available choices from **Support > Advertising Privacy > Privacy and cookie settings**.

Purchasing **Remove Ads** disables rewarded and interstitial advertisements in Life Empire. Google may retain information already processed under its own retention policies.

For more information about Google's data practices and controls, see:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google advertising controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/

## In-app purchases

Life Empire uses **Google Play Billing** for optional gem packs and permanent ad removal. Google processes the purchase and payment information required to complete the transaction. Life Empire and Snelroy do not receive or store your complete payment-card number, bank-account details, or Google account password.

The game may receive and store a product identifier, purchase status, and purchase or transaction identifier. These are used only to deliver the purchased content, restore permanent entitlements where supported, and prevent the same transaction from being granted more than once.

For more information, see the Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=0.privacynotice

## External links and services

Life Empire can open an invitation to the Life Empire Discord community and can open web pages or an email application for privacy and support. Discord, GitHub, email providers, browsers, and other external services are independent services with their own terms and privacy practices. Information you choose to provide to those services is handled by their providers and is not controlled by this policy.

Discord's privacy policy is available at https://discord.com/privacy.

## Information we do not intentionally collect

Life Empire does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect or transmit to a Life Empire server your email address, contacts, precise location, photos, videos, voice recordings, government identifiers, payment-card details, or financial account credentials. Gameplay involving salaries, stocks, loans, property, and businesses is fictional and does not connect to real financial accounts.

## Legal basis and choices

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, local processing is necessary to provide the game features you request. Advertising data is processed by Google according to the consent choices and other legal bases presented through Google's consent system. You may decline optional advertising consent where that choice is offered, change available advertising privacy choices in the Support screen, purchase Remove Ads, or stop using and uninstall the game.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google for advertising, consent management, Play distribution, purchases, security, and diagnostics as described above. Information is also shared when you deliberately open or communicate through an external service. We may disclose information if required by law, but because we do not operate an account or gameplay-data server, the information directly available to us is limited.

## Security

Life Empire minimizes data collection by keeping game saves on your device and not requiring an account. Communications with Google and linked third-party services use the security protections provided by those services, including encryption in transit where supported. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Retention and deletion

- Local game information remains on your device until you delete the relevant save, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
- Purchase-entitlement and duplicate-fulfillment information remains locally stored for as long as the app data remains on the device.
- Google and other third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.
- Because Life Empire does not provide an account or store gameplay information on a Snelroy server, there is no separate server-side Life Empire account to delete.

For privacy questions or requests relating to information directly controlled by Snelroy, email **lifeempireapp@gmail.com**. For information controlled by Google, Discord, or another provider, you may also need to use that provider's privacy controls or contact process.

## Children's privacy

Life Empire is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a Life Empire account or server. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided information directly to us can contact **lifeempireapp@gmail.com**.

## International users

Google and other third-party providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Their privacy policies explain the safeguards used for international transfers. Local save information remains on your device unless it is processed by the operating system, device backup service, or another service you have enabled.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Life Empire, applicable law, or its service providers change. We will publish the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the game or its store listing where appropriate.
